# Recruitment texts

Draft, version 1, 4 November 2026. Recruitment starts only after ethics approval. The proposal (Table 2) plans to recruit early through student societies, such as the music and dramatics societies. Every text gives the contact address and says that taking part is voluntary.

## Message for society groups and email lists

> **Singers wanted for a short study on auto-tune (5 × 20 minutes)**
>
> We are final-year CS students at COMSATS Lahore, and we have built AdaptiTune, a pitch-correction program that learns how each singer likes their voice corrected. We are looking for singers of any level, beginners welcome, aged 18 or over, to try it.
>
> You would come to 5 short sessions of up to 20 minutes each, on different days over about four weeks. In each one you sing three short phrases of your choice, listen to corrected versions through headphones, say which you would keep, and try setting the correction yourself.
>
> Your recordings stay on our project laptop under a code, never online and never with your name. Taking part is voluntary, and you can stop at any time.
>
> Interested? Contact Afzaal Afzal at [university email]. This study has been approved by [ethics committee, reference].

## Screening questions (asked before booking session 1)

1. Are you 18 or older? *(must be yes)*
2. Do you sing, at any level? *(must be yes)*
3. Do you know of any hearing loss? *(must be no)*
4. Can you come to five sessions on different days within about four weeks? *(must be yes)*
5. Have you worked on AdaptiTune or helped choose its settings? *(must be no)*

Anyone who answers differently is thanked and told they are not eligible this time. Their answers are not kept.
