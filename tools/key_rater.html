Key rater

Label the key of each excerpt for the AdaptiTune key-estimation test (O2). Works offline; nothing leaves this computer.

How to label
  1. Type your rater name, then choose the excerpt folder made by adaptitune key-excerpts (it holds excerpts.json and an audio folder).
  2. Listen to the whole excerpt. Find the tonic, the note that sounds like "home".
  3. Pick a key and switch the drone on: the right tonic sounds settled under the melody, a wrong one rubs. Play scale checks major against minor (the third decides).
  4. If there is no clear tonal centre, or the excerpt changes key, choose No clear key.
  5. Label on your own and do not compare with the other rater until you have both exported. Your work is saved in this browser as you go; Export CSV when you finish.
